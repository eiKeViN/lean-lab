No assumption silently supplies this missing conclusion. The Z module's log reports failed rewrites and module-instance mismatches in the coordinate/cycle identifications, a wrong binder type at line 382, and unresolved boundary-dimension steps; `strip_zDim` therefore earns draft credit only.

For E, the failures include the repetition projected-kernel proof at lines 218/223 and the full-coordinate projection proof at line 260.
